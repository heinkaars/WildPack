WildPack support

Last updated: 9 November 2026

WildPack helps you photograph wildlife, find out what you saw, and build a life list. It is made by an independent developer, Hein Kaars Sijpesteijn.

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas? Email hein.wildpack@gmail.com. I usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

WildPack got the animal wrong. Why?
Identification is done by AI and works best with a clear, close photo of one animal. Look through the other possible matches on the result screen, or tap "Not quite, try again" and take another photo. Never rely on WildPack to decide whether an animal is safe to approach or touch.

Identification or Ask doesn't work.
WildPack needs an internet connection to identify animals, answer questions and make the daily quiz. Check your connection and try again.

How do I keep my life list on a new phone?
Create an account with your email: tap your picture on Home to open your profile, then tap "Create an account". On the new phone, choose "I have an account" and sign in.

How do I delete my account?
Open your profile and tap "Delete account". This removes your account, your photos and all your data from our servers.
