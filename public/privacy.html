WildPack privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

WildPack helps you photograph wildlife, find out what you saw, and build a life list. This page explains what information the app uses, why, and what you can do about it. WildPack is made by an independent developer, Hein Kaars Sijpesteijn, who is responsible for your data. You can reach me at CONTACT_EMAIL.

What WildPack stores

  • Your account. When you open WildPack it creates an account without asking for anything. If you choose to create an account with email, we store your email address and a password (encrypted).
  • Your profile. The name you choose, the docent you pick, your streak and your XP.
  • Your sightings. The photos you take or choose, the species, when you saw it, and a place name if you type one in.
  • Your Ask chats. The questions you ask about a species and the answers.
  • Your quiz scores. One score per day you play.
  • Usage limits. To stop abuse, the server briefly counts requests per account and per internet (IP) address. These counts are deleted automatically within a short time.

WildPack does not use your location, does not show ads, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell your data.

AI identification and Ask (OpenAI)

WildPack uses OpenAI to identify the animal in a photo, to answer your Ask questions and to write your daily quiz. Before the first photo or question is sent, the app asks for your permission.

  • For identification we send the photo. For Ask we send your question, the earlier messages in that chat and the species name. For the quiz we send species names from your life list.
  • We never send your name, email address or account details to OpenAI.
  • OpenAI processes this to give an answer. Under its API terms it does not use this data to train its models, and it may keep it for up to 30 days to detect abuse before deleting it.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase stores your account, profile, sightings, photos, chats and scores.
  • OpenAI provides the identification, Ask answers and quiz questions, as described above.
  • Expo (EAS Hosting) runs the small server that connects the app to OpenAI.

These services process data on our behalf and only for running WildPack. Some of them are based in the United States, so your data may be processed there, under the safeguards these companies provide (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

Why we use it

We use your data to provide the app you asked for: keeping your life list, identifying animals and answering questions. Sending photos and questions to OpenAI is based on your permission, which you give in the app.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. Your sightings, photos and chats also stay on your phone until you delete the app or log out.

Deleting your data and your rights

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. This removes your photos and all your data from our servers straight away.

You can also ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how it's used, by emailing CONTACT_EMAIL. If you live in the EU, you can also complain to your national data protection authority (in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Children

WildPack is not aimed at children under 13. If you are under 16 and live in the EU, ask a parent or guardian before creating an account with your email.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. For bigger changes we'll tell you in the app.
